Corpus attribute distributions

Real values measured over 2,000 random 256-token windows from the REMI training split (giga-midi, 137,177 songs). The shaded band marks roughly mean ± 2σ — targets inside it are well-covered by training data; targets outside it ask the model to extrapolate, which is where guided generation tends to degrade.
