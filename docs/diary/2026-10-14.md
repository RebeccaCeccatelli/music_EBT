  steps, but the optimum is still exactly k = 2: they do not make extra steps help. So the fixed 2-step
  training (and the reused step-2 embedding for k ≥ 3) is the main reason. Caveat: stabilized is 12.6k steps
  less trained and has a worse loss (1.130 vs 1.117), so compare shapes, not levels.
- Damage grows with training length in the original lineage (+0.010 at 55.5k → +0.019 at 88.8k): the model
  specialises further to the 2-step path.
- **Overconfidence confirmed**: entropy falls after step 2 while CE rises (sharper, not more accurate).
  Calibration gap CE − entropy, k=2 → 16: original +0.051 → +0.085, stabilized −0.007 → +0.021
