## Mechanism of EBT's asymmetric steering: measured
- New `eval/guidance_mechanism.py` (job 25384607, 31 s). It computes (1)
  the random-token value of each attribute, i.e. `compute_*` on uniformly
  drawn attribute tokens, and (2) the normalized entropy of the
  attribute's values per continuation vs λ, against the unguided level and
  the AR methods at max strength.
- In 5/5 attribute×tokenizer cases the "easy" direction points from the
  baseline to the random-token value (velocity 0.674→0.509 down; REMI
  duration 0.047→0.498, pitch 0.356→0.511, Ant duration 0.025→0.502 up).
  Ant pitch has baseline ≈ random value (0.467 vs 0.499), and both
  directions work there.
- EBT value entropy roughly doubles with λ (0.24→0.73 velocity, 0.27→0.58
  duration, 0.36→0.62 / 0.47→0.76 pitch). AR methods at max strength:
  0.22–0.46 (PPLM highest, +0.1–0.18 over unguided).
- Conclusion: strong EBT guidance flattens the attribute-token choice. That
  drives the value toward the random-token value (the asymmetry) and
  produces random-note dissonance. Added to
  `thesis_findings/2026-10-09_guidance_ebt_vs_ar.md`.

## Literature check on novelty
- Web search plus `papers/`: no PPLM application to symbolic music, no
  Energy-Based Transformer for music/MIDI/audio, no gradient guidance of
  autoregressive or energy-based *token* models for symbolic music (only
  diffusion). Closest prior work: activation steering of the Multitrack
  Music Transformer on pitch/duration (arXiv:2605.31295), SMITIN and
  MusicRFM (audio). Written up in
  `thesis_findings/2026-10-09_novelty_literature_check.md`.
- Reminder for the thesis: our PPLM baseline is "PPLM-style" (one
  logit-gradient step; no KV perturbation, KL term or fusion).

## Demo: baseline guidance + data-driven λ ranges
- Single Attribute variants now have a "Model & method" choice: EBT energy guidance, Llama PPLM, Llama/GPT-2 tilting (REMI only), Llama/GPT-2 best-of-N. Same mechanics as `ar_guidance_sweep.py` (PPLM via `attribute_target`/`lambda_attribute`/regressor on the baseline's own embeddings, paired with the selected checkpoint; tilt via `ExpectationTilt` logit processor; best-of-N = N unguided draws, keep the closest by the rule-based attribute). Strength slider re-ranges per method.
- Fixed: every Single Attribute generation failed with `NameError: ckpt_path` (introduced in 3a22ce5; the row step used an undefined name instead of `state["ckpt_path"]`).
- EBT λ ranges are now per tokenizer × attribute, from `final_remi.json` / `final_ant_ar.json` (range = accuracy ≳ 0.75 → edge of the quality budget; default = best accuracy within budget):

  | | range | default | why |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | REMI velocity | 0.02–0.07 | 0.03 | 0.97 acc; still in budget at 0.07, out at 0.09 |
  | REMI duration | 0.01–0.055 | 0.02 | 0.85 acc; in budget up to 0.055 |
  | REMI pitch_register | 0.01–0.03 | 0.02 | 0.79 acc; 0.04 already cacophonic (diss 0.19, overlap 0.48) |
  | Ant duration | 0.002–0.008 | 0.005 | 0.78 acc; falls toward chance above ~0.01 |
  | Ant pitch_register | 0.004–0.015 | 0.01 | 0.76 acc; leaves budget at 0.012–0.015 |

  Old demo ranges let REMI pitch_register reach 0.07 and put every Ant attribute in the cacophony zone (0.015–0.07). Compose's shared λ (never swept): REMI 0.01–0.05 default 0.02, Ant 0.002–0.012 default 0.005 (was 0.04 for both).
- Why attribute-dependent: velocity doesn't touch harmony, so it tolerates strong pushes; pitch register changes which notes sound together and breaks harmony fast. Why tokenizer-dependent: Ant needs ~4× less λ even though λ is normalised to the EBT's own gradient norm.
