## Steering-direction and cacophony plots
- New `eval/plot_guidance_sweeps.py steering`: per attribute, one column
  per method. Top row: progress toward target = (achieved − baseline) /
  requested change, by push direction, with 95% prompt-bootstrap bands.
  Bottom row: harsh dissonance vs strength with the real band, the
  unguided level and the random-note level. Figures:
  `thesis_findings/figures/guidance_final/{remi,ant}/steering_*.png`.
- Findings (added to `2026-10-09_guidance_ebt_vs_ar.md`):
  - Sign accuracy overstates best-of-N: progress is only ~0.1 (Ant) to
    ~0.3 (REMI) at N=16.
  - AR methods move symmetrically but undershoot (tilt up to ~1.0, PPLM
    ~0.4–0.75).
  - EBT is the only method that reaches or overshoots targets, but only
    one way per attribute: up for duration/pitch (down ≈ 0, or the wrong
    way for Ant duration), down for velocity. Mechanism: a
    target-sensitive effect plus a direction-independent drift at high λ
    (Ant push-down ends at 0.12 > baseline 0.026).
  - REMI pitch_register also reaches random-note dissonance at λ ≥ 0.04.
